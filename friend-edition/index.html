Rare Heist is a turn-based stealth heist in a one-bit cutaway building, starring a Rare Friends Generations NFT drawn from its original on-chain pixels. This edition runs inside FriendSDK v0.1.2, so it opens only for a wallet that holds a Friend.

DOOR 1OPEN FRIEND EDITIONNEEDS A GENERATIONS FRIEND, GEN 1 OR HIGHER

FriendSDK v0.1.2 edition: needs a browser wallet on Robinhood Chain (4663) holding a Generations Friend. The SDK checks ownership before the game loads, then your Friend walks every lesson and job.

 DOOR 2PLAY THE FULL GAME AS A GUESTNO WALLET NEEDED

Every lesson and job plus the shared Last Heist vault, the Workshop and the Studio. Play a guest Friend, preview any Friend by its token ID, or connect a wallet to play your own.

Behind door 1

  • 5 lessons and 14 solo jobs, the same engine and levels as the full game.
  • Wallet, Friend choice and the ownership check belong to the FriendSDK runtime.
  • No purchases, rewards or token actions. Progress lasts for the session.

Behind door 2

  • Guest play, no sign-up: start with a one-minute lesson.
  • Last Heist: one shared vault on a server that replays every route.
  • DEMO RF (play money) by default. Real RF only in the opt-in LIVE BURN.
