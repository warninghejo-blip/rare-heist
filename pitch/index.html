    F4Character Spotlight

    Your Friend is the thief

    Connect a wallet and Rare Heist finds the Generations Friends you own. The one you pick walks into every building, with its own walking frames, its #ID and its family. It is never redrawn and never recoloured.

    F4Read from Robinhood Chain

    Bit for bit, from the chain

    Each frame is one 256-bit word from the Rare Friends registry: 16 rows of 16 pixels, one bit each. The game asks familyOf, then seedOf, then frames(family, seed), and draws what comes back.

    F2Last Heist, the shared vault

    One vault
    Every clear changes it

    Players take turns rewriting one building on the game server. The last thief standing when the clock runs out wins the round.

    1. Clear the vault. Beat the current version clean.
    2. Add one obstacle. Drag a wall, laser or camera from the side palette onto the building.
    3. The solver checks it live. Your piece must break the last winning route, and the vault must still be beatable.
    4. Prove it. Clear your own version. The server replays every route with the same engine.
    5. Hold the lead. Whoever cleared last when the clock ends takes the round.

    Why the numbers hold

    • Skill pays. In an n-entrant round a player who wins more than 1/(0.7n) of the time comes out ahead: 29% with five entrants.
    • Self-dealing always loses. Faking a rival with a second session stakes 100 and gets 70 back.
    • The server keeps the books. Every settle checks stakes = paid + burned + refunded + held. No negative balances, one stake per player per round, idempotent settlement.

    Playable now in DEMO RF on the Last Heist server: 50 a stake from a 200-a-day guest wallet. Nothing real is staked, paid or burned there.

    From DEMO RF to real RF

    1. DEMO stake roundsSame rules, 70/30 split, refunds and ledger checks, in play money.
    2. An audited escrow contractPays only the winning Friend, the burn or a refund. Refunds and claims can never be paused.
    3. One Friend, one entryStakes made on chain for an owned Friend, checked with ownerOf.
    4. Results anyone can checkA proof bundle anyone can replay with the open engine, and a guardian veto.
    5. Legal reviewPaid-entry skill contests are regulated differently from place to place.
    6. Real-RF stake rounds70% to the winning Friend's own wallet, 30% to 0x…dEaD in the settlement transaction.

    Steps 2 to 6 are designed in the escrow spec, not deployed. Read the spec

    No open findings

    What is real and what is DEMO

    • Stake rounds run in DEMO RF. Nothing real is staked, paid or burned there.
    • Rare Heist pays out no RF: no faucets, no play-to-earn, no real prize.
    • The shop burns real RF. First real burn: 10 RF (tx 0xcf6b…3957). The ledger shows every burn live.
    • The real-RF escrow is designed, not deployed or audited.
    • Difficulty is tuned with a solver and has not been playtested with players yet.
